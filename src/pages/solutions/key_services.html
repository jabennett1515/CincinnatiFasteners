Services

Professional Fastening and Supplying Services

Cincinnati Fasteners provides tailored fastening and supply solutions to streamline operations, reduce costs, and enhance efficiency. From custom labeling to vendor-managed inventory, we deliver reliable and timely services to meet your business needs.

  • Special Labeling

    Packaging of parts with your internal part number, barcoded and readable, to monitor traceability and simplify invoicing.

  • Custom Packaging

    Multi-part kits assembled and labeled to your exact specifications, saving labor and cost to receive, pick.

  • Priority Delivery

    A fleet of trucks to assure timely delivery of urgent and time sensitive orders along with convenient to-your-door service.

  • Secondary Processing

    Pre-assembly of kits of varying sizes of bolts, nuts, screws, washers and other fasteners commonly used in your operation.
