About Us

Cincinnati Fasteners has been a trusted name in the fastener industry for over 50 years. Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has made us a leader in the field. We specialize in providing high-quality fasteners and inventory solutions to meet the needs of our clients across various industries.

Our Story

Over 50 Years of Experience

Cincinnati Fastener was founded in 1971 by John C. and Linda Huetcher. After serving his country as a Green Beret in the U.S. Army, John was encouraged by Linda to start a business of their own. Together, they built a small Mom-and-Pop fastener distribution company rooted in integrity, hard work, and exceptional service.

In the mid-90s, their sons John and Eric Huetcher took over day-to-day operations, carrying forward the mission with fresh energy and vision. Over the past 30+ years, the company has grown steadily—earning new business by staying true to core values: teamwork, dependability, integrity, respect, and world-class customer service.

But the real driver of growth? Culture. By fostering a workplace that values people just as much as performance, Cincinnati Fastener continues to attract the best talent in the fastener industry.
