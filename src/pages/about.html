Cincinnati Fasteners

Our Story

Over 50 Years of Experience

Cincinnati Fasteners was founded in 1971 on the principles of quality, integrity, and service. Over the decades, we’ve grown from a small regional supplier into a trusted name in the fastener industry. Our mission has always been simple: to provide reliable products and personalized support that exceed expectations.

Throughout our history, we’ve aligned ourselves with reputable manufacturers, continuously expanded our inventory, and invested in our team’s expertise. Our commitment remains steadfast—we don’t just sell fasteners, we solve fastening challenges.
