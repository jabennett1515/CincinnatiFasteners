Why We're The Right Fit

Cincinnati Fastener and Industrial Supply opened its doors in 1971 and grew with the intention of becoming one of the most respected companies in the Fastener industry. Our dedicated & experienced team is here to assist you with all your assembly component needs. We specialize in custom hardware, as well as all your standard fastening requirements.

To be the industry leader and your trusted provider of custom and standard hardware, we use a wide range of value-added services and the latest technologies. This enables our business partners to achieve extraordinary and sustained success. We achieve this success by continually training our employees, using best-in-class products and our world-class data driven VMI System.

With over 50 years’ industry experience, we offer value add/value engineer assistance, documented cost reductions as well as our robust ISO 9001 Registered Quality Management System.
